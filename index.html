🌙 新月事件詳細介紹 / New Moon Events Info

  • 1. 時之沙漏 / Hourglass of Time 時間倒流！雙方體力產生震盪，當前血量直接恢復/調整至各自最大生命值的 50%。 Time flows backward! Directly sets both players' current HP to 50% of their maximum HP.
  • 2. 破滅之咒 / Curse of Ruin 夜空中降下毀滅詛咒，使雙方同時失去當前生命值的 20%（無視防禦）。 A destructive curse descends, causing both players to simultaneously lose 20% of their current HP (ignores defense).
  • 3. 神聖新星 / Holy Nova 新月綻放溫暖的光芒，雙方同時恢復各自最大生命值的 30%。 The new moon emits a warm glow, allowing both players to restore 30% of their maximum HP.
  • 4. 狂暴之月 / Frenzied Moon 激發骨子裡的嗜血狂暴，雙方的能量（EN）直接全部補滿達 10 點。 Ignites the bloodlust within, immediately filling both players' energy back to full (10 dots).
  • 5. 枯竭風暴 / Depletion Storm 元素寂滅的風暴席捲戰場，使雙方當前的能量（EN）全部歸零。 A storm of element annihilation sweeps the arena, draining both players' current energy to 0.
  • 6. 命運天秤 / Scales of Fate 命運交織！當前生命值百分比較低者，將會與對手的生命值百分比直接對調。 Destiny intertwined! The player with the lower HP percentage swaps their HP percentage with their opponent.
  • 7. 極寒永夜 / Eternal Frost Night 絕對零度的永夜降臨，雙方同時獲得 2 層「深層凍結（Deep Frost）」狀態。 An absolute-zero polar night falls, granting both players 2 stacks of Deep Frost status simultaneously.
  • 8. 神明沉默 / Divine Silence 任何凡人不可稱聖，雙方身上所有的增益與減益狀態（護盾、反傷、標記、凍結、暈眩等）全部清除。 No mortal shall be blessed. Clears all buffs and debuffs from both players.
  • 9. 烈焰風暴 / Blaze Storm 混亂的烈焰席捲戰場，對雙方同時造成 25 點真實傷害（無視防禦）。 Chaotic embers sweep the arena, dealing 25 pure damage to both players simultaneously.
